SHARED CALENDAR

Shared calendar

Share your calendar with family, a partner or coworkers. Both of you can add and edit plans.

  • Show them only "Busy" and keep the details to yourself
  • Your period, todos and anniversaries are never shared
  • Multi-day trips show as one continuous bar
PRIORITY MATRIX

Priority matrix

Sort todos by importance and urgency, and see at a glance what to do first.

  • Switch between list and matrix in one tap
  • Checked items move to Completed, so nothing gets lost
  • Check things off right from the home screen widget
VOICE ALARM

Voice alarms

Say one sentence to set an alarm or add a plan. No date pickers, no time wheels.

  • Voice input understands Chinese today, with English on the way
  • Date ranges become multi-day events
  • Rings full screen, right on the lock screen

FAQ

Good to know before you download

Is it free?

It is free to download, and alarms, calendar, todos and voice input are free to use. Some features, like anniversaries and week view, need a subscription. Workouts and cooking timers have usage limits on the free plan.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. OwnITime is on Android, available on Google Play.

Does the other person need OwnITime to share a calendar?

Yes. Once you both have it, scan a QR code to connect. After they accept, they can see the plans you share.

Can my partner see my period?

No. Sharing covers calendar events only. Your period, todos, alarms and anniversaries are never shared.

Which languages does voice input understand?

Chinese today. English voice input is on the way. Recognition is more accurate online and falls back to on-device recognition offline.
